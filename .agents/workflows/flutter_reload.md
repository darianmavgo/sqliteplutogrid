---
description: Hot Reload/Restart Flutter App
---

To perform a Hot Reload or Hot Restart while the app is running:

1. **Hot Reload (fast, preserves state)**
   - Click into the terminal running `flutter run`.
   - Press `r`.

2. **Hot Restart (full state reset)**
   - Click into the terminal running `flutter run`.
   - Press `R` (Shift+R).

3. **Detach/Quit**
   - Press `d` to detach.
   - Press `q` to quit.
